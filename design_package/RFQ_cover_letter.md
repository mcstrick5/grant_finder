## Project

Retrofit module for a standard folding aluminium bedside commode that lets a caregiver empty the waste pan
while the user remains seated. The round pan slides 20" (508 mm) straight out the BACK of the chair on two
ball-bearing drawer slides, lifts out for emptying, and slides back to a latched position under the seat.

## Key requirements
```
  - Retrofit: clamps to the existing frame, no welding or drilling of the chair.
  - Pan and carrier concentric with the 440 mm round seat; pan must lift freely out of the carrier at full extension.
  - Design load 300 lb (136 kg) seated user; 15 kg in pan at full extension; no tipping with pan extended.
  - Pan, lid and carrier smooth, non-porous, dishwasher/disinfectant safe (PP or 304 SS).
  - Slides and hardware stainless or zinc-plated with sealed bearings; washable.
  - Tolerances per drawings (+/-1.0 mm untoleranced); all edges deburred.
```
